Common questions

Answers to what clients ask first.

Do you work with businesses outside of your immediate area?

Yes. While we're rooted locally and believe in hands-on service, we support clients across the region with a mix of on-site visits and remote management. We'll be upfront if geography changes what we can do.

Are you tied to any specific hardware or software vendor?

No. We're vendor-agnostic. We recommend the right tool for your environment, budget, and goals — not whatever earns us the highest margin. That independence is core to how we build trust.

How is pricing structured?

Every engagement is quoted with a clear scope and fixed pricing — whether it's a single wireless survey or a multi-site deployment. No surprise invoices and no hidden change orders.

Can you work alongside our existing IT team or provider?

Absolutely. We often serve as a specialist extension of in-house IT — handling wireless, network architecture, deployments, or security assessments — or as a trusted second opinion. Collaboration is the norm, not the exception.

What happens after a project goes live?

We document everything, train your team, and offer ongoing support plans. We don't disappear after go-live — most of our clients have worked with us for years.

Not sure which services fit your business?

Start with a no-pressure consultation. We'll review your environment and share clear, honest recommendations.
